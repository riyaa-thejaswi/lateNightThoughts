NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

Between the Lines

A small collection of reasons for putting pen to paper when the world has gone quiet.

Why I write

Late Night Thoughts started as a quiet notebook on my nightstand. It was never meant to be grand or fully polished—just a sanctuary for fragments of memory, soft questions, and truths that felt too delicate to say out loud during the rush of daytime.

Writing in the dark has a peculiar kind of honesty. When everyone else is asleep, the filters soften, and you can finally hear whatever has been quietly waiting in the corners of your mind.

On solitude & warmth

I am drawn to quiet things: rain tapping against windowpanes, old piano keys, cold tea left forgotten while reading, and the unspoken weight of everyday longing.

If you have arrived here feeling a little tired, or looking for a mirror to your own quiet thoughts, I hope these stanzas feel like a calm hand to hold. You are always welcome to linger as long as you need.
