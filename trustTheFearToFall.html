LATE NIGHT THOUGHTS

Trust the Fear
to Fall

A quiet reflection on confronting the uncertainty of letting go, finding peace within vulnerability, and discovering that surrender is where real strength begins.

Fear of heights,

Fear of spiders,

But what about the fear of love?

Fear of falling,

Fear of heights,

But never the fear of trusting the wrong "one".

Because how do you know,

Know if the warmth in their tone,

Know if the heart they show,

Is really the place that you can call home.

Know if the "always"

Is truly forever

Or instead just a promise,

That will one day be broken.

There’s fear of the silence,

When the talking is done.

Fear of the “always”

Turning to "none".

For in love there will always be fear

That our "better" is not their "best",

That our story is only their chapter.”

But we must let the heights,

Put our courage to the test,

For we cannot find said love

Without leaving the nest.

So we trade in the spiders

For the web that we weave.

Learning to trust,

What we choose to believe.

That beyond the falling

And fear of the fall,

Is the love that we deserve,

The greatest height of all.

WRITTEN IN THE DARK — R.
