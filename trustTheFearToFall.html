LATE NIGHT THOUGHTS

Trust the Fear
to Fall

A quiet reflection on confronting the uncertainty of letting go, finding peace within vulnerability, and discovering that surrender is where real strength begins.

There is a ledge inside the night,

Where every breath grows thin.

And all the voices soften down,

To the quiet held within.

You count the reasons not to leap,

The bruises you might keep.

The way the ground has never asked,

How far the drop, how deep.

But fear is not the enemy,

It is the pulse of care.

The proof that something matters still,

Enough to hold you there.

So trust the fear to teach your feet,

The shape of letting go.

To open, weightless, into dark,

And find you learned to grow.

For falling was the only way,

The sky could hold you tall.

And what you feared would break you first,

Was only teaching you to fall.

WRITTEN IN THE DARK — R.
